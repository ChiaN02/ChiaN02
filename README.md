## 👩‍💻 About me

I'm a Biomedical Engineering graduate, currently finishing my Master's degree in **Artificial Intelligence and Data Engineering** at the University of Pisa.

My background combines engineering and biomedical fundamentals with machine learning and data analysis, and I enjoy working where these fields overlap.

- 🎓 Master's in AI and Data Engineering, University of Pisa
- 🩺 Bachelor's in Biomedical Engineering, with a thesis on brain signals (fNIRS and GSR)
- 💡 Most of my projects work with health data: fetal monitoring, Parkinson's, brain synchrony
